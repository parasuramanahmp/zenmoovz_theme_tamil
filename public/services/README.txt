Real footage for the three in-store services.

Drop your own files here with EXACTLY these names:

  knock.mp4    + knock.jpg      Bat knocking-in   (mallet on the blade)
  engrave.mp4  + engrave.jpg    Bat engraving     (name going onto the bat)
  string.mp4   + string.jpg     Badminton stringing (racket being strung)

- .mp4 plays silently on a loop, only while the card is on screen (H.264, no audio needed, aim for under 3 MB, 5 to 10 seconds, landscape 5:3 e.g. 1000x600).
- .jpg is the still shown before the video loads, and the whole picture if you only have a photo (leave out the .mp4).
- Anything missing falls back to the animated drawing, so you can add them one at a time.
- Visitors with "reduce motion" switched on see the .jpg instead of the video.
To use different names or folders, edit SERVICE_MEDIA in src/data/services.js.

Related photos (optional), shown as a small row under each service's main picture on the Services page:
  knock-1.jpg  knock-2.jpg  knock-3.jpg
  engrave-1.jpg  engrave-2.jpg  engrave-3.jpg
  string-1.jpg  string-2.jpg  string-3.jpg
Square-ish photos work best. Any that are missing are simply left out; with none, the row does not appear.
